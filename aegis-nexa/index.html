AEGIS NEXA

Advanced water purification and recovery infrastructure.

AEGIS NEXA is designed around efficient physical and electrochemical water treatment. It combines advanced filtration, electrochemical treatment, recovery, intelligent control and a cartridge-based architecture in an energy-conscious engineered system. Scroll to take it apart.

AEGIS NEXA · Cutaway sequence

01 / 08≈1500 × 600 × 650 mm

Exterior

A compact engineered pod. The enclosure houses treatment, recovery, cartridges and control in one serviceable unit — designed as water infrastructure rather than an appliance.

02 / 08

Internal layers

Panels separate to reveal the internal architecture: pre-filtration, a pressure-lock / refill vessel, the treatment stack, two cartridges, the control module and the air system.

03 / 08

Water enters

Source water enters through the inlet and is conditioned in the pre-filtration stage before reaching the pressure-lock / refill architecture.

04 / 08

Treatment stages

Water moves through the advanced electrochemical / composite treatment stack — the stage where the AENXIC science meets engineered hardware.

05 / 08

Recovery

A recovery pathway returns a stream for further treatment rather than discarding it. Recovery is approximately 90–94%.

06 / 08

Clean-water output

Treated water passes through the cartridge stage to a dedicated outlet. Throughput is up to 48,000 litres per day.

07 / 08

Cartridge architecture

Two cartridges lift out for service. The bundle concept includes two cartridges per year, keeping maintenance predictable.

08 / 08

JARVIS control layer

The control module connects to every stage. JARVIS integration is intended to bring monitoring, control and engineering intelligence to the system.

01 System concept

Design characteristics, explained as engineering — not adjectives.

No conventional high-pressure pump dependency

The core concept is designed to avoid depending on a conventional high-pressure pump.

Pressure-lock / refill architecture

A sealed vessel stage that locks and refills in cycles, managing pressure within the system.

Air harvesting / air replenishment

An air subsystem that replenishes the air volume the architecture relies on.

Solar-assisted air replenishment

Exploring solar assistance for the air-replenishment cycle.

Two-cartridge architecture

Two serviceable cartridges carry the treatment media; the bundle concept includes two per year.

Advanced electrochemical / composite treatment

Treatment built on electrochemical processes and composite materials — the AENXIC foundation.
