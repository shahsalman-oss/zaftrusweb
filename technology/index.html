03 Questions

What people ask first

Is it reverse osmosis?

No. AEGIS NEXA is designed around low-energy electrochemical and composite-material purification rather than conventional high-pressure membrane systems.

Is JARVIS a chatbot?

No. It is an engineering AI and control system: its outputs are parameters, geometry and validated engineering actions, not conversation.

Where is the work done?

Zaftrus is based in Srinagar, Kashmir, with prototype fabrication through a manufacturing partner and support from national institutions.
