AI.01 Engineering intelligence

JARVIS.From intent toengineering execution.

Not a chatbot. An engineering AI and control system that turns what an engineer means into what a system does.

01 Engineering Execution Graph

Eight stages from intent to action

01Human intent

Plain language, sketches or requirements.

02Semantic understanding

Objects, relationships, quantities, purpose.

03Engineering reasoning

Domain knowledge and trade-offs.

04Constraints

Loads, tolerances, materials, standards.

05Parametric representation

Editable parameters and relations.

06CAD / geometry

Parameters drive CAD operations.

07Validation

Checked against constraints.

08Execution

Files, operations, fabrication steps.

02 In practice

What a request looks like

An engineer states a requirement. JARVIS parses it, applies domain reasoning, makes the constraints explicit, produces an editable parametric model, drives CAD to create geometry, and validates the result before anything is accepted.

Every stage is inspectable, so a mistake can be corrected at the level where it was made.

 execution-graph · illustrative
# illustrative — not a recorded session
intent      "Mounting bracket, 120 mm, two M6 holes, aluminium"
semantic    part=bracket length=120 holes=2×M6 material=aluminium
constraints edge_distance ≥ 1.5·d  tolerance ±0.1
parametric  length, hole_pitch, thickness  → editable
geometry    CAD operations  → STEP
validate    constraints checked  ✓

03 Architecture

Four cooperating layers

01

Parser layer

Turns requests and documents into structured engineering objects.

02

Planner layer

Decides which engineering steps are needed and in what order.

03

Engineering layer

Applies calculations, constraints, repair and provenance tracking.

04

CAD layer

Produces real geometry and exports engineering files.

04 Positioning

A chatbot versus an engineering system

A general chatbot
  • Produces text from a prompt
  • Constraints stay implicit
  • Output is hard to verify
  • Typically cloud-dependent
JARVIS
  • Produces parameters, geometry and files
  • Constraints are explicit and checked
  • Deterministic validation before acceptance
  • Designed local-first
